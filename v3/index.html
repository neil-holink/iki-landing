    Frequently asked questions

    How does AI routing actually work?
    i.ki evaluates each click's device, geo, language, referrer, and behavioral signals against your rules, then sends the visitor to the destination most likely to convert — adjusting automatically as performance data accumulates.
    Is i.ki GDPR compliant?
    Yes. i.ki is GDPR-ready with EU data processing, a DPA available on request, and we never sell personal data.
    Can I use my own custom domain?
    Absolutely. Connect a branded domain (e.g. go.yourbrand.com) on Pro and above, with automatic SSL provisioning.
    Do you have an API?
    Yes — a full REST API plus webhooks let you create links, fetch analytics, and automate your workflows.
    Can I migrate from Bitly or Rebrandly?
    Yes. Import your existing links and custom domains in a few clicks — your old links keep working throughout.
    What uptime can I expect?
    99.99% uptime, backed by a global edge network and a formal SLA on Enterprise plans.
